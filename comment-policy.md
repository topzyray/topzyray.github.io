<!-- # Comment Policy -->

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

Comments are welcome on this website. The purpose of the comment section is to encourage constructive discussion, exchange of ideas, questions, and professional collaboration.

By submitting a comment, you agree to follow this policy.

## 1. Be Respectful

Treat other readers, authors, and participants with respect.

Do not post comments that contain:

- Personal attacks or harassment
- Threats or intimidation
- Hate speech or discriminatory remarks
- Abusive, insulting, or deliberately hostile language
- Defamatory allegations presented as fact
- Content intended to shame, humiliate, or target another person

Disagreement is acceptable. Personal attacks are not.

## 2. Keep Comments Relevant

Comments should contribute meaningfully to the topic being discussed.

Comments may be removed when they are:

- Completely unrelated to the article
- Repetitive or excessively disruptive
- Posted solely to promote another website or service
- Intended to derail the discussion
- Automated or generated through spam systems

If you have a question about a specific article, please keep the discussion reasonably related to its subject.

## 3. No Spam

Spam is not permitted.

This includes:

- Unsolicited advertisements
- Repeated promotional messages
- SEO-oriented link dropping
- Affiliate links posted without meaningful context
- Automated comments
- Comments posted primarily to obtain backlinks
- Promotional content disguised as a discussion

Relevant links may be permitted when they provide genuine value to the discussion.

## 4. Links and Self-Promotion

You may reference your own website, project, GitHub repository, article, product, or professional work when it is relevant to the discussion.

However, comments should not be used primarily as a means of advertising or acquiring backlinks.

The site owner may remove promotional links or comments that appear primarily intended for marketing, SEO, or traffic generation.

## 5. Technical and Security Discussions

This website may contain articles about software engineering, backend development, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and related technical subjects.

Technical disagreement and criticism are welcome.

However, comments should not contain:

- Malicious code intended to harm other users
- Malware or ransomware
- Credentials, API keys, passwords, tokens, or other secrets
- Private or personally identifiable information belonging to another person
- Instructions intended to facilitate unlawful activity
- Deliberate attempts to exploit or compromise this website

If you discover a security vulnerability affecting this website, please do **not** disclose sensitive technical details publicly in the comments. Use the appropriate private security-contact channel instead.

## 6. Accuracy and Responsibility

You are responsible for the content of your comments.

Do not knowingly post false information about individuals, organizations, products, or services.

Comments represent the opinions of their authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the site owner.

## 7. Personal Information

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information in comments.

Do not publicly share:

- Home addresses
- Telephone numbers
- Email addresses belonging to other people
- Identification numbers
- Passwords or authentication credentials
- Financial information
- Private correspondence
- Other sensitive personal information

If you accidentally publish sensitive information, contact the site owner as soon as possible so appropriate action can be considered.

## 8. Moderation

Comments may be moderated at the discretion of the site owner.

A comment may be edited, hidden, or removed when it violates this policy or when moderation is otherwise necessary to maintain a useful discussion environment.

The site owner is not obligated to publish every submitted comment.

Moderation decisions may be made without prior notice.

## 9. Disqus

This website may provide comments through **Utterances** (which stores discussions as GitHub issues) and **Disqus** (which provides guest commenting when enabled). These are third-party services with their own terms and privacy practices. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information in comments, and review the relevant service policies before participating.

When you participate in discussions through either service, your interaction is also subject to that service's own terms, privacy practices, and policies.

Please review Disqus's policies before submitting a comment if you have concerns about how your information or comments are handled by the service.

## 10. Copyright and Third-Party Content

Do not post copyrighted material that you do not have permission to distribute.

Short quotations may be appropriate where legally permitted and should normally be accompanied by attribution and, where appropriate, a link to the original source.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This comment policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, commenting system, applicable requirements, or moderation practices.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

## 12. Contact

If you believe a comment violates this policy, contains sensitive information, or requires moderation, please contact the site owner through the contact information provided on this website.
